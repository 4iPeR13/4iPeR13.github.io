Sudoku Realms – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Sudoku Realms ("the app") is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the app uses and what happens to it.

What stays on your device

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your game progress, settings (sound, theme, intro animation) and play-time counters are stored only on your device. We do not collect, receive or sell this data.

Advertising

The app shows ads between games, provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use data from your device, such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow it), IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More on how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads start appearing only after you have finished a few games. The game itself works the same with or without personalized ads.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK): if you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy options.
  • Tracking (iOS): the app may ask permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can say no, or change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.
  • Limit ad tracking: you can reset or restrict your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. Deleting the app removes all game data from your device. Data held by Google is governed by Google's policy and your Google/iOS privacy controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
